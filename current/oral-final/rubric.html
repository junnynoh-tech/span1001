Oral Final Rubric

8 questions: 2 warm-ups (¿Cómo te llamas? ¿De dónde eres?) and 3 from each of your two topics. I count five things over those 8. Pick the number in each row to see the grade; hover over or tap a category for what it counts.

Points for each count. Choose one number per row.
Category012345678+Grade
20 pts · count of questionsHow many of the 8 questions you understand the first time, without a repeat. Asking ¿Puede repetir? is fine: it only costs this one.
25 pts · count of sentencesHow many answers are a full Spanish sentence with a verb: ¿Qué estudias? → Estudio biología. A word or phrase doesn't count.
20 pts · count of errorsHow many answers have a wrong form of what the question asks for (¿Duermes? → duermo, not duermes). At most one per answer. An answer in English, no answer or “No sé” counts as one.
20 pts · count of answersHow many answers add a detail or a reason: Estudio biología porque es interesante. A follow-up you answer that way counts too (once per question).
15 pts · count of wordsHow many words I can't understand because of how they're said. An accent is fine. An answer with no Spanish counts as one.
Total
How the counting works
  • One repeat, one phrase, one error or one unclear word still gets full points.
  • An answer with no Spanish (English, no answer, No sé) counts as an error and as a word I couldn't understand.
  • A memorized answer that doesn't answer the question doesn't count as understood or as an answer.
  • A follow-up answered with a full sentence that adds something counts as saying more (once per question).
  • Fixing your own mistake isn't an error. No Spanish at all, or absent: 0.
  • Not counted against you: Asking ¿Puede repetir? or ¿Cómo se dice…?: it's a strategy (a repeat counts only in Understanding). An accent, as long as the word is understood. Mistakes in grammar beyond Chapters 1–5.
