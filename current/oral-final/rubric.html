Oral Final Rubric

8 questions: 2 warm-ups (¿Cómo te llamas? ¿De dónde eres?) and 3 from each of your two topics, counted over all 8. Choose a box in each row to see your grade. Hover over or tap a category to see what it measures.

Oral Final rubric, 100 points
CategoryStrongGoodDevelopingBeginningGrade
20 pointsWhether you understand each question the first time, without a repeat. Asking ¿Puede repetir? is fine: it only affects this category.
25 pointsWhether you answer with a full Spanish sentence that has a verb: ¿Qué estudias? → Estudio biología. A single word or phrase isn't a sentence.
20 pointsThe form each question asks for: ¿Duermes? → duermo, not duermes. ¿Tienes…? → Tengo… An answer in English or “No sé” counts as an error.
20 pointsAdding a detail or reason: Estudio biología porque es interesante. Answering a follow-up (¿Por qué?) with a full sentence that adds something counts too.
15 pointsWhether I can understand your words. An accent is fine. An answer with no Spanish counts as a word I couldn't understand.

How the counting works
  • An answer with no Spanish (English, no answer, No sé) counts as an error and as a word I couldn't understand.
  • A memorized answer that doesn't answer the question doesn't count as understood or as an answer.
  • A follow-up answered with a full sentence that adds something counts as saying more (once per question).
  • One error per answer at most. Fixing your own mistake isn't an error. No Spanish at all, or absent: 0.
  • Not counted against you: Asking ¿Puede repetir? or ¿Cómo se dice…?: it's a strategy (a repeat counts only in Understanding). An accent, as long as the word is understood. Mistakes in grammar beyond Chapters 1–5.
