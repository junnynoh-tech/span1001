SPAN 1001 · Examen Oral

Oral Final: Practice Questions

A one-on-one conversation in Spanish with your instructor, at your sign-up time (the dates are on the course calendar). Topics come from Chapters 1–5. These are possible questions: practice answering them aloud.

Download the PDF, bring it to the Language Lab and practice with a lab assistant. How it’s graded: the Oral Final rubric.

How to practice with a lab assistant

  1. The lab assistant asks questions in any order, mixing the chapters. On the oral final, they won’t come in order either.
  2. Answer in a full sentence, then add one more thing with y, pero, porque or también.
  3. The lab assistant asks a follow-up: ¿Por qué? ¿Y tu familia? ¿Y tu mejor amigo? Answer that too.
  4. Do a second round with no notes. Tick ✓ each question you can answer without looking.
  5. Don’t memorize a script. Practice answering, so new questions don’t surprise you.
If you get stuck, say it in Spanish
SpanishEnglish
¿Puede repetir, por favor?Can you repeat, please?
Más despacio, por favor.Slower, please.
¿Cómo se dice ___ en español?How do you say ___ in Spanish?
No sé.I don't know.
No entiendo.I don't understand.

How it’s graded

Five categories, 100 points, counted over the 8 questions. Here’s what full points look like. The rubric has every level.

Full points in each category
CategoryPointsFull points (Strong)
Understanding the questions20Understands 7–8 of the 8 questions the first time.
Answering in Spanish, in sentences25Answers 7–8 of 8 with a full Spanish sentence that has a conjugated verb (Estudio biología).
Accuracy of the forms asked for200–1 errors in the forms the questions ask for (¿Duermes? → duermo; ¿Cómo son? → son altos).
Saying more20Adds a detail or reason (y, pero, porque, también, a second sentence) in 5 or more answers.
Pronunciation: being understood15Easy to understand; 0–1 words I couldn't understand.

An answer in English or No sé counts as an error. Asking ¿Puede repetir? only affects Understanding, and an accent is fine.
